The original dictionary license in LICENSE applies to this data. ListenFlow's top-level MIT license is unchanged. No third-party Python dictionary wrapper is included. The reader uses Python's standard library; no new runtime dependency or network resource download is required.

The inventory comprises 39 ARPABET base phones with vowel lexical stress 0/1/2. Alternatives are retained in original order without ranking or probability. Broad IPA is a display mapping of dictionary phones, not observed speech. Dictionary coverage and accuracy are limited, especially for names, other languages, and novel spellings.
